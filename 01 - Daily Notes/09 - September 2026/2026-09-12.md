### Decisions Made

- **Not rebuilding for a smaller ISO.** At 4.79 GB it is bigger than tiny11's advertised
  ~3.5 GB, because this release exports `install.wim` at max compression instead of
  converting to `install.esd`. Shrinking it means an elevated `/compress:recovery` re-export
  and another long DISM pass for no practical benefit. The only real consequence is that
  `install.wim` at 4.11 GB exceeds the FAT32 file limit, which Rufus handles on its own.
- **Renamed the broken ISO folder instead of deleting it.** Deleting is Nathan's call; the
  name is enough of a guard in the meantime.

## Session 8, 8:40 AM: voice line run, and a Whisper loop fixed

### What Got Done
- **The voice line ran end to end on the rebuilt machine** for the first time. The 09-04 "BRAIN CONNECT failed (exit code 129)" did not recur: the brain connected in 21 seconds. First run downloaded Whisper `base.en` and Kokoro-82M (cache was wiped with the machine); Kokoro then stalled loading at 0.3 GB of available RAM until Nathan closed Obsidian. It spoke the greeting and answered turns.
- **"hey beast" was heard but not accepted.** `base.en` transcribed it as "how you beast" and "bye beast" (0.778 against the 0.80 bar). `wake_phrase_fuzz` set to **0.76** on Nathan's OK (not 0.75: that would let "a beast" at 0.750 through). `wake_debug` turned **off**: it had been logging every room conversation the open mic picked up into `backtalk.log`.
- **Push-to-talk turns came through as "Okay. All right. All right..." whatever Nathan said.** Diagnosed by measurement rather than guessing: mic level (quiet, about -40 dBFS, but a plain recording transcribed correctly and 8 dB of gain changed nothing), Caps Lock (a 3 s hold read as 2.99 s), and the speaker plus open-mic streams (same level, and the first twelve counted numbers came through before the loop). Cause: backtalk calls Whisper with `temperature=0.0`, which switches off faster-whisper's re-decode of looping output. Fixed on Nathan's OK with a one-line change to `ears.py` `transcribe()` (temperature fallback), tested on the recording that looped, and committed as local patch `cd7198b`. The bundle and patch backups in `tool-configs` were rebuilt and re-tested to restore.

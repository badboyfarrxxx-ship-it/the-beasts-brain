# Chrysalis book notes

Research notes on RinoZ's *Chrysalis* series (nine books so far, ongoing), compiled
2026-09-19 for [[colony-game]]. They were compiled from public recap pages, fan wiki
entries, publisher blurbs and reader reviews, not from the audiobook itself, and they are
summaries rather than text from the books. They are the source material for the personal
build. The sale build uses none of the names in here.

**Spoilers throughout.**

How reliable each part is: Books 1–5 come from a detailed chapter recap, so they are solid.
Books 6–9 come from blurbs and a few reader comments, so they are thin. Details are in the
Books 4–9 part.

## Books 1–3 (the box set)

**Spoiler warning:** covers the whole box set (Books 1–3), including the Book 3 ending.
Names are the book's. The sale build replaces all of them (see the last section of this part).

---

### 1. Premise

- Anthony, a teenager on Earth, dies and is reborn as an ant monster in Pangera, a game-like world.
- A voice he nicknames "Gandalf" (the System) introduces him. He has stats, levels, skills and evolutions.
- The Dungeon runs through the whole planet, in layers called strata. Everything living has access to the System.
- Anthony starts as a lone Hatchling Worker, about a meter long, separated from his colony.
- The story is about him finding the colony, then growing it from a small nest into an army and a civilisation.

### 2. Core mechanics from the books (use these as game systems)

**Levels and evolution**
- Killing monsters gives XP and levels.
- Level 5 unlocks an evolution choice. Level 10 unlocks the next tier, and more options appear if he has absorbed a special core.
- Anthony's line: Hatchling Worker → Mature Worker (doubles his size, gives a pheromone gland) → Mind Ant (tier 3, gravity magic, a second brain for auto-grinding skills) → Dispersed Mind Ant (tier 4, third brain) → Juvenile Colony Paragon (tier 5, powered by the colony).
- He usually picks the well-rounded option over the flashy specialist one.

**Skills**
- Gained at level-up and improved by using them. Repeat use is how they rank up, so grinding is a real mechanic.
- Extra brains let him train skills passively.

**Biomass and mutations**
- Eating gives Biomass, which is spent on body mutations: better vision, stealth, stronger jaws, speech via pheromones, and so on.

**Monster cores (the big system)**
- Monsters drop cores. Cores come in grades: normal, special, and rare (rare is stronger than special).
- Anthony can condense his own core, which unlocks magic. Absorbing other cores pushes his core's limit. Overloading it causes pain and, at the extreme, risk of bursting.
- Cores can be reconstituted into a loyal pet who is bound to him.
- Cores can be fused together into a stronger special core, and given custom evolution paths, using core-manipulation skills.

**Mana**
- Higher-tier monsters need more ambient mana to survive. At tier 4, Anthony can't live indefinitely near the surface, where mana is low. This pushes the player deeper as they grow.

**Dungeon wave**
- For weeks, monsters spawn endlessly from the walls. It is the main timed threat in Books 1–3. Everyone either flees to the surface or holds a fortress.

### 3. The colony

- **Queen:** egg-laying rate rises with the XP and Biomass the colony brings in.
- **Pheromones:** the ants' language. Anthony upgrades it from vague impressions to full speech. At first only the Queen can answer fully.
- **Pitfall traps:** Anthony's early way to hunt with fewer casualties. He links them into the nest so workers can harvest them.
- **Monster farms:** set up for safe XP and Biomass for new hatchlings.
- **Egg redesign ("the day that changed Pangera"):** with the Queen's permission, Anthony reworks her core. The new eggs are smarter and speak pheromone language, but weaker physically, slower to lay and slower to hatch. Evolution paths split into three branches:
  - Soldiers (including leaders and scouts)
  - Support (including queens)
  - Artisans (including mages)
- **New species:** the first twenty of the new ants hatch and the System labels them a new species, Formica Sapiens. Anthony converts the whole colony to it and they can now speak.
- **Personality quirk:** the new ants are dangerously eager to die for the colony, and Anthony spends time teaching them to stay alive. One, named Leeroy, struggles with it.
- **Aphid Queen:** Anthony fuses aphid cores into a template and the ant Queen reconstitutes it. The aphids are a farmable, renewable food source.

### 4. Companions (the party)

| Name | Origin | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tiny | Small monkey, reconstituted from a gorilla's core | Fist fighter, lightning attack, reckless in battle |
| Vibrant | An ant hatchling born with a core, rescued during the wave | Trained by Anthony, learns pheromone language, evolves into a Greater Soldier (strength, command aura, speed) |
| Crinis | Reconstituted from the core of a Crinis Inanis, a monster that is a mouth with shadowy tentacles | Has no normal sight or hearing, senses magic, very strong against swarms of weak monsters. Gets a custom "Special Death Ball" evolution |

### 5. Enemies and threats (in the order you meet them)

1. Tunnel monsters near the nest (early XP)
2. The Abyssal Legion, a human force with soldiers and mages that patrols the Dungeon and treats ant swarms as a serious threat
3. A giant crocodile and giant gorilla dueling near the colony; a larger crocodile with a special core
4. A Crinis Inanis attacking the colony's tunnel to the surface
5. Hostile monsters around the surface forest
6. Liria's Queen and her guards: a job to help her retake her throne that ends in a double-cross from both sides
7. A garden expanse of hostile plants
8. A marsh expanse full of the crocodile boss's offspring, including a highly evolved one with a fear aura that drops a rare core
9. **Garralosh**, the giant crocodile boss (fire affinity, herself a reincarnator), leading hordes across the surface
10. Ka'armodo, a lizard-like being that is one of her allies and later watches Anthony instead of fighting him

### 6. Allies, neutrals and places

- **Sophos:** a peaceful race that specialises in customising cores to make pets. Formosus ("Formo") is the one Anthony meets.
- **Branchies:** reclusive creatures that fuse with trees. They show Anthony where to dig into the Dungeon and provide mana-infused water that helps monsters form cores.
- **Human refugees and village:** Liria's kingdom falls to the wave, and survivors settle near the ants. A priest named Beyn leads them and comes to see the ants with awe. An adventuring party led by Morrelia becomes an uneasy ally.
- **Locations:** the original nest deep in the Dungeon, the church on the surface where they break out, the forest, the new nest site, the garden expanse, the marsh expanse, the surface village.

### 7. Story order (a natural campaign structure)

**Book 1: Find the colony and escape the wave**
1. Wake up as a Hatchling Worker, hunt weak monsters, dodge the Legion.
2. Follow a pheromone trail to the colony. Reach level 5, condense a core, steal a core stash from a human camp, evolve.
3. Join the colony, help against the crocodile and gorilla, get Tiny.
4. Build the pitfall trap network, learn speech, reach tier 3 as a Mind Ant.
5. Meet Formo and learn about the wave. Fight the Crinis Inanis on the way up.

**Book 2: Life on the surface**
1. Break out into the church, settle in the forest with the branchies.
2. Get Vibrant and Crinis. Deal with Liria's Queen and steal the core payment.
3. Grind deeper, reach tier 4, redesign the eggs.
4. Take in refugees, clear the marsh, and set up aphid farming.

**Book 3: Smarter ants and the boss fight**
1. Hatch the first Formica Sapiens generation and convert the colony.
2. Build monster farms and the Aphid Queen.
3. The smarter ants ignore his pheromone barriers and abduct the human village, planning to let the Queen kill them for XP. Anthony stops it and uses it to tell the humans the colony is now sapient.
4. Garralosh and her army march on the colony. Guerrilla strikes, then a final stand in the anthill.
5. Anthony overloads on a special and a rare core, evolves to tier 5, and beats Garralosh with gravity magic.
6. Ending hook: the Ka'armodo tells him about a cult that wants a monster strong enough to descend to the core of the world.

### 8. Game ideas that map well

- **Idle / colony builder (my recommendation):** dig the nest, assign ants to hunting, farming and research. Run the egg-redesign trade-off (smarter vs stronger vs faster) as a real choice. Unlock tiers by hitting XP milestones.
- **Boss timers:** dungeon waves as timed events that push the player to prepare.
- **Party system:** three named companions with different roles and growth paths.
- **Core crafting:** fuse and reconstitute cores into new units.
- **Risk mechanic:** overloading the core for a big evolution, with a chance to fail.

### 9. If you ever want to sell it

Keep the *systems* (tiers, cores, egg tuning, waves, monster evolution) and change the names, characters and world. Game mechanics aren't protected the way characters, names and setting are. Personal use is fine with everything as is.

### 1. The world: strata (dungeon layers)

| Stratum | What it's like | What lives there |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Where the story starts (my reading: the garden and marsh expanses are also here). Tunnels; low mana near the top | Ordinary monsters, the crocodile spawn, aphids |
| 2nd | Darkness, cold and poison | Golgari (see below), the mysterious "Grey" |
| 3rd | Ash and fire | Demons, who live in cities built on huge rock spires that hold up the world above |
| 4th | Water and light, described as glittering. Large cities, kingdoms and empires on enormous mountains | Termites (bred by Ka'armodo), the One Tree, and the trading partners of Book 9 |

Deeper strata have more mana. A monster needs to go deeper as its tier rises.

### 3. New mechanics and story systems

- **"The Call":** a pull to go deeper. Granin says it means the Ancients have noticed him.
- **The Ancients:** nineteen exist, and Anthony is being groomed as the twentieth. They are waking up.
- **Reincarnators:** other humans reborn as monsters. Known ones: Garralosh (dead), James (giant earthworm), Sarah (bear), and the One Tree.
- **Registering as a civilised race:** "Grey" files the ants as a protected people, which stops the Golgari and Legion from simply exterminating them.
- **Colony expansion:** extra nests across the first two strata, an underground city captured (Rylleh) to shut down the Legion's portal gates, and a force of ten thousand elite ants moved through a portal.
- **Colony as an empire:** Brixin, a demon city lord, is hired as manager of conquered demon cities. Later, Book 9 is about **trade and commerce** on the 4th stratum.
- **Champions:** elite ants trained by Anthony. A protégé named **Brilliant** appears in Book 7.
- **Dungeon cycle:** the System says it is peaking. Waves keep coming.

### 5. Enemies and bosses in order

1. **Golgari ambush and death tournament** (2nd stratum). Anthony is captured and forced to fight other candidates. Reincarnators James and Sarah are there.
2. **Envy Demon** (magic specialist), defeated in the tournament, becomes Invidia.
3. **Legion siege** of the nest. Titus can't be beaten head-on, so the ants stall and then use a hostage (Morrelia) as leverage.
4. **Golgari assault and betrayal.** James sneaks into the brood chambers with Golgari troops. Sarah helps repel it. This sets up Anthony's revenge on James.
5. **Demon city war** (3rd stratum). The ants take Roklu after killing its lord and win a war against Orpule.
6. **Termite war** (4th stratum). The termites are bred by Ka'armodo to kill the Tree's creatures. Anthony fights alongside ten thousand elite ants and destroys the termite mountain with a lava bomb. This gives him tier 7.
7. **Arconidem's uprising** (Book 8). Tier 8 Heralds attack across the 3rd and 4th strata, and the colony is first on their list.

### 6. Book-by-book, as far as I could confirm

- **Book 4:** the wave ends. Anthony goes down to the 2nd stratum, is captured by the Golgari, enters the tournament, and escapes when the colony arrives.
- **Book 5:** Golgari and Legion attack together. Colony builds new nests and takes Rylleh. "Grey" steps in. Anthony hits tier 6.
- **Book 6 (blurb only):** Anthony enters the 3rd stratum, with many demons, new foes and rapid growth of his family. This matches the demon-city material above.
- **Book 7 (blurb, plus one reader):** territory, nests and Champions. He trains Brilliant, tries to avoid war with the Ka'armodo, and helps the Mother Tree against termites. A reader notes the pets don't appear in this book.
- **Book 8 (blurb, plus readers):** Anthony is a Mythic monster. Arconidem wakes, and his Heralds attack. Readers say the termite and demon storylines wrap up, the colony's other ants get more focus, and there is a comedic "Tunnel Ball" match and a champion trained under Anthony's guidance. The colony calls Anthony "the Eldest" (my reading).
- **Book 9 (blurb only):** the termite threat is over, "probably". The colony goes on a trade mission through the cities and kingdoms of the 4th stratum.

### 7. What this adds for the game

- **Stratum progression:** each new stratum as a new world with its own hazard (cold and poison, fire and demons, water and light), rising mana needs and new enemy types.
- **Civilisation phase:** after the early monster-hunting loop, the colony moves into **conquest and trade**, with captured cities run by a hired manager. This is a natural mid-game to late-game shift.
- **Diplomacy:** getting recognised as a civilised race changes who can attack you.
- **Big choice at tier 7:** power now or a risky transformation.
- **Faction relationships:** allies (One Tree, Sophos), rivals (Legion, Golgari) and betrayers (James, Liria's queen from Book 2).
- **Hostage / leverage events:** Morrelia's capture in Book 5 as a scripted diplomatic moment.
- **Timed events:** the wave and the awakening of Arconidem as escalating threats.
